Product Requirement & Build Brief: Anti-Gravity MVP Engine
1. Executive Problem Statement & Scope Definition
"High-stakes clinical, medical, and health records (Category D) stored in consumer cloud galleries fail catastrophically during time-critical health management because existing multimodal search models rely on conversational chat paradigms, generic OCR optimized only for planar black-and-white documents, and flat chronological libraries that interleave sensitive personal media with professional/health assets."

Methodological Scope & Segmentation Note:
This MVP intentionally focuses on the high-consequence sub-segment of personal health management—where retrieval carries immediate medical risk, such as identifying the correct medication dosage, finding historical lab results (HbA1c), or verifying prescriptions at a pharmacy.

When patients, caregivers, or doctors attempt retrieval under direct scrutiny in clinics or pharmacies, current solutions (Google Photos / "Ask Photos") break down across three fatal friction points:
	Semantic Cross-Contamination: Natural language and object queries (e.g., "Metformin") contaminate health search grids with domestic family photos (e.g., family dinners, vacation selfies), causing acute embarrassment and cognitive overload.
	Text-on-Texture Blindness: Existing OCR engines fail to index non-planar, low-contrast, or highly reflective surfaces—such as metallic blister packs for pills, handwritten doctor scripts, or faded thermal pharmacy receipts.
	Conversational Latency & Thumbnail Blindness: Conversational chat interfaces consume 40%+ of mobile viewports with conversational prose and compress search results into single-row horizontal carousels (8–10s latency). Standard 1-inch thumbnails force high-friction modal tap-and-zoom inspection cycles.

2. Product Vision & MVP Objective
Build "Health Cabinet Lens" (Anti-Gravity Core): An AI-native, multimodal retrieval feature embedded within a Google Photos–style media container that turns photo galleries from a passive nostalgic scrapbook into a high-density, zero-latency clinical evidence audit engine.

The MVP must enable an external evaluator to successfully execute the "October 2024 Metformin Blister Pack Retrieval" and the "August 2024 Metropolis Lab Report" within 10 seconds, demonstrating:
	Zero cross-domain leakage from family/domestic media.
	Instant visual legibility of pharmaceutical salts on reflective blister packs.
	Sub-100ms UI response without conversational chat overhead.

3. Core Functional Requirements (The 4 AI-Native Pillars)
Pillar 1: Autonomous Ingest Partitioning (Health vs. Personal Quarantine)
	Domain Quarantine: High-entropy clinical captures are isolated into a dedicated "Health Cabinet." Queries executed within health contexts strictly exclude domestic/personal images from candidate search results.

Pillar 2: Substrate-Aware Visual OCR (Non-Planar Text Extraction)
	Texture & Marker Tuning: Detect non-standard, low-fidelity, handwritten text:
	Specular metallic blister packs (foil reflections).
	Handwritten cursive on doctor scripts.
	Faded thermal paper (pharmacy receipts).
	Surface Classification: Tag images with substrate attributes (Silver Foil, Doctor Script, Thermal Paper) to enable instant multi-modal facet narrowing.

Pillar 3: Dynamic Evidence-Centered Cropping (Thumbnail Telemetry)
	Attention-Crop Previews: When a query matches a detected medicine (e.g., Metformin 500mg ER), the thumbnail preview dynamically crops and centers directly on the localized bounding box of that evidence.
	Inline Verification Badges: Render detected alphanumeric tokens as high-contrast overlay pills directly on thumbnails in the grid view.

Pillar 4: High-Density Triage Canvas (Zero-Chat UX)
	Dense Multi-Column Grid: 3-to-4 column vertical scroll layout on mobile/desktop, utilizing 100% of vertical screen space.
	Telegraphic inputs: Support direct token queries, material facets (Silver Foil), and reference image inputs.
